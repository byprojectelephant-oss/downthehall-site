## Titles, skills, levels, and overlap decisions
1. **Save for a Backpack**
   - Topic/skills: Money | Track a savings goal.
   - Difficulty: Developing: equal additions and subtraction.
   - Relationship: New gap: accumulating savings over weeks; unlike grocery budgets or automatic-payment reserves.

2. **Choose a Print Job Within Budget**
   - Topic/skills: Math | Multiply page costs.
   - Difficulty: Developing: repeated addition with cents.
   - Relationship: New service-cost gap: page quantity times rate; adjacent to price comparisons, with a print-settings decision.

3. **Prepare for a Library Card Visit**
   - Topic/skills: Functional reading | Match document requirements.
   - Difficulty: Developing: combine two requirements.
   - Relationship: New library-access subskill: selecting supporting documents; distinct from due dates and computer reservations.

4. **Follow a Service Desk Queue Ticket**
   - Topic/skills: Community independence | Match number and location.
   - Difficulty: Foundational: locate explicit details.
   - Relationship: New gap: service queue codes and counter matching; not appointment-time or schedule reading.

5. **Find an Accessible Entrance - Level 2**
   - Topic/skills: Community safety | Apply a route constraint.
   - Difficulty: Level 2: compare routes and request help.
   - Relationship: Intentional extension of Neighborhood Map Route Choices: Level 2 adds accessibility constraints and a blocked-route contingency.

6. **Decline an Invitation - Level 2**
   - Topic/skills: Social communication | Express and respect a choice.
   - Difficulty: Level 2: compose a reply and maintain a boundary.
   - Relationship: Intentional extension of Friendship Skills: Level 2 moves from a word bank to generating a refusal, optional alternative, and repeated boundary.

7. **Store Glasses in Their Usual Place**
   - Topic/skills: Personal care | Protect and locate a personal item.
   - Difficulty: Foundational: follow a stated care plan.
   - Relationship: New personal-care subskill: protective storage of glasses; adjacent to hygiene kits and device charging, but a different item-care routine.

8. **Check a Grocery Substitution - Level 2**
   - Topic/skills: Practical life skills | Compare a replacement with a request.
   - Difficulty: Level 2: check three conditions.
   - Relationship: Intentional extension of Grocery Budget Basics and Compare Package Sizes Level 2: Level 2 transfers comparison to substitution consent using flavor, quantity, and a price ceiling; no unit-price calculation.

9. **Use a Coat Check Claim Ticket**
   - Topic/skills: Functional reading | Connect a claim code to belongings.
   - Difficulty: Foundational: read and retain a claim ticket.
   - Relationship: New transaction subskill: retain a claim token for intentionally stored belongings; unlike Collect a Lost Item, identification is through a service ticket.

10. **Plan a Quiet Break and Return - Level 2**
   - Topic/skills: Routines | Coordinate a break with a return time.
   - Difficulty: Level 2: time subtraction and self-advocacy.
   - Relationship: Intentional extension of Calming Strategies and Self-Advocacy: Level 2 adds an agreed duration, return location, and renegotiation; learner-selected support, not a compliance task.

## Design and use

Art is decorative and not necessary to answer. Learners can write, speak, use AAC, or dictate; a calculator is allowed. Levels describe task demand rather than age. Accept equivalent answers; sample requests are not the only correct phrasing.
