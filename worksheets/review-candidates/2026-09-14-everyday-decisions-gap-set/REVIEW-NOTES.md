## Candidates and gap decisions

1. **Automatic Payment Balance Plan - Level 2**
   - Topic and skills: Money | Subtraction and planning.
   - Difficulty: Level 2: two-step money math.
   - Existing-topic relationship: Progression from Debit Card Balance Check at the Pharmacy: reserves future automatic payments and a minimum balance; two-step Level 2 planning.

2. **Check a Reservation Deposit**
   - Topic and skills: Money | Part payment and balance.
   - Difficulty: Developing: subtraction within 30.
   - Existing-topic relationship: New subskill: subtracting an advance deposit from a total; adjacent to Make Change from 10 Dollars, but focuses on part payments.

3. **Book a Haircut - Level 2**
   - Topic and skills: Personal care | Compare availability and request a service.
   - Difficulty: Level 2: coordinate two constraints.
   - Existing-topic relationship: Intentional Level 2 extension of Read an Appointment Card: selects an appointment using availability and cost, then formulates a request.

4. **Ask for Written Directions - Level 2**
   - Topic and skills: Social communication | Request and confirm support.
   - Difficulty: Level 2: request, then confirm.
   - Existing-topic relationship: Intentional Level 2 extension of Self-Advocacy (Speak Up for What You Need): specifies a communication format, confirms the first step, and repairs a remaining misunderstanding.

5. **Borrow a Charger and Return It**
   - Topic and skills: Social communication | Permission and responsibility.
   - Difficulty: Foundational: explicit details and short request.
   - Existing-topic relationship: New scenario and subskill: borrowing permission, an agreed return time, and renegotiation; adjacent to general friendship skills.

6. **Collect a Lost Item**
   - Topic and skills: Functional reading | Match evidence and describe belongings.
   - Difficulty: Developing: compare multiple details.
   - Existing-topic relationship: New gap: lost-property identification and an evidence-based claim; distinct from package delivery notices.

7. **Read a Water Refill Station Notice**
   - Topic and skills: Community independence | Follow a public-use notice.
   - Difficulty: Foundational: locate explicit instructions.
   - Existing-topic relationship: New real-world notice: shared refill equipment and spill reporting; adjacent to community signs, with a new procedural reading task.

8. **Plan a Device Charging Routine**
   - Topic and skills: Routines | Put preparation steps in order.
   - Difficulty: Foundational: read an explicit sequence.
   - Existing-topic relationship: New routine: preparing an assistive device for daily access; adjacent to morning routines but focused on device readiness.

9. **Pause Before an Unexpected QR Code - Level 2**
   - Topic and skills: Safety | Recognize pressure and verify independently.
   - Difficulty: Level 2: apply a safety rule to a new medium.
   - Existing-topic relationship: Intentional Level 2 extension of Phone Scam Awareness: transfers warning-sign recognition to an unexpected package and QR code, with independent verification.

10. **Follow a Changing-Room Privacy Routine**
   - Topic and skills: Personal care | Privacy and requesting help.
   - Difficulty: Foundational: apply posted rules.
   - Existing-topic relationship: New personal-care context: shared changing-space privacy and chosen assistance; distinct from bathing sequences and online privacy.

## Design and use

Reuses existing SchoolArt PNG illustrations (embedded directly in editable HTML), retaining pastel fills, warm sketch outlines, soft shadows, and a friendly school style. Art is a modest decorative cue, not an answer dependency. Letter pages have 0.4-0.48 inch margins, large writing areas, dark text, and light fills. Learners may answer orally, by pointing, with AAC, or by dictation. Levels describe task demand, not grade or age. Model using a separate example so the four practice items remain available.
