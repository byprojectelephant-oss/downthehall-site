What this worksheet supports

Pick the Right Hygiene Item gives learners a focused opportunity to practice functional reading, decision-making, communication, routines, or money skills in a practical scenario. Learners can write, point, dictate, or use AAC as appropriate.

How to use it

  1. Print the full PDF for a worksheet with its answer key, or choose the learner-only edition.
  2. Preview unfamiliar words and read the scenario together before answering.
  3. Connect the practice activity to a real routine when it is useful for the learner.

Who it is for

This resource can support teens and adults in special education, transition programs, adult day services, community programs, supported living, and home learning.
