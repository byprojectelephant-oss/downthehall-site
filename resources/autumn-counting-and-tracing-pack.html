Autumn Counting and Tracing Pack

Eight illustrated worksheets: coin counting, autumn shopping, six-word dotted letter and word tracing, numbers 0-10, autumn missing letters, and an optional Halloween puzzle.

How to use it

Print the pages you want. Learners can write, speak, point, sign, or use a communication device. Work at a comfortable pace and use the picture clues for support.

The Halloween activity is optional. The letter pages use dotted tracing with six illustrated words per page.
